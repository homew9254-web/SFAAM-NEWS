Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites function properly and provide information to site owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

SFAAM NEWS uses cookies to:

  • Essential: Enable core functionality like security and accessibility
  • Preferences: Remember your theme and font size settings
  • Analytics: Understand how visitors interact with our website

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Session Cookies: Temporary cookies deleted when you close your browser.
Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted.
Third-Party Cookies: Set by analytics providers (privacy-focused, no personal tracking).

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

5. Contact

For questions about our Cookie Policy, contact editorial@sfaamnews.com.
