📰 What is SFAAM NEWS?

SFAAM NEWS is an independent, digital-first news platform founded in 2024 by Shamsulhaq and headquartered in Bagrot, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. The platform covers six regions — World, USA, UK, Pakistan, India, and Germany — with the same depth of editorial seriousness, treating no region as an afterthought. Every article is written from scratch in SFAAM’s editorial voice, never republished from a wire feed, and never plagiarized from a source.

The platform runs on a proprietary three-agent AI journalism pipeline: a Fact Extractor reads trusted RSS sources and converts each story into structured JSON facts (who, what, where, when, why, numbers); an Editor agent then writes an editorial plan based on those facts; finally, a Journalist agent writes the published article using only the facts and the plan — never the original source text. This pipeline guarantees originality at scale while keeping every claim traceable to a verified source.

For readers, this means fast, original, and well-structured journalism on every story — with modern features like TL;DR quick summaries, fact-check status badges, audio narrations, dark mode, and a Progressive Web App that works offline.

👥 Editorial Team

Our editorial team combines experienced journalists with the proprietary SFAAM AI pipeline. Every article is reviewed against the editorial standards below before publication.

⚖ Editorial Ethics

Independence. SFAAM NEWS accepts no political funding and is editorially independent of all commercial and governmental interests. Funding decisions never influence story selection or framing.

Accuracy. Every story carries a visible fact-check badge — Verified, Under Review, or Community Fact-Checked. The badge is updated as more verification becomes available, so readers always know the current status of what they are reading.

Fairness. Controversial stories present multiple perspectives. SFAAM NEWS does not take sides in partisan disputes, and opinion is clearly separated from reporting.

Transparency. Errors are corrected promptly and visibly. See our Corrections Policy for every change made after publication.

Originality. Every article is written from extracted facts — never copy-pasted from a source. This is enforced at the pipeline level: the Journalist agent literally never sees the source text, only the structured facts.

✅ Fact-Checking Standards

1. Source Verification — All RSS sources are individually vetted. We monitor hundreds of trusted outlets across six regions, including BBC, Reuters, AP, CNN, Al Jazeera, Dawn, Times of India, The Guardian, and Deutsche Welle.

2. Structured Fact Extraction — Each source article is converted into a JSON fact sheet (who, what, where, when, why, numbers). Only verified facts enter the writing pipeline.

3. Multi-Source Cross-Referencing — Major claims are cross-referenced against multiple independent sources. Single-source claims are flagged with the Under Review badge.

4. Visible Status Badges — Every article displays its current verification status: ✓ Verified, ⚠ Under Review, or 🔎 Community Fact-Checked.

5. Community Input — Readers can flag concerns through the Contact page. Flagged articles are re-reviewed and either upgraded, corrected, or removed.

⚡ Reader-First Features

  • ⚡ TL;DR Quick Summary — a 3-bullet overview at the top of every article, with a toggle between Quick Summary and Deep Read modes.
  • ✅ Fact-Check Badge — visible verification status on every story.
  • 🎧 Audio Narration — text-to-speech audio for hands-free reading (rolling out across regions).
  • 📰 Reading Progress — a progress bar at the top of every article showing how far you’ve read.
  • 📝 Font Size Controls — three font sizes for accessibility.
  • 🌝 Dark / Light / Sepia Themes — reader-chosen, persists across sessions.
  • ⭐ Bookmarks — save articles for later, stored locally on your device.
  • 👍 Likes & Comments — engage with stories without an account.
  • 📱 PWA / Offline Reading — install SFAAM NEWS as an app and read cached articles offline.

🌍 Our Mission

SFAAM NEWS exists to make quality global journalism accessible to everyone. We believe staying informed should be easy, fast, and free. Our mission is to be the go-to source for people who want to stay updated on world events without spending hours searching across multiple websites or wading through paywalls.

We cover six regions not because six is a magic number, but because together they represent the major centers of global political, economic, and cultural influence — from Washington and London to Islamabad, New Delhi, and Berlin, with a World desk that catches everything else. Every region is staffed and edited with the same care, so a reader in Karachi gets the same depth of coverage on Pakistan as a reader in Berlin gets on Germany.
