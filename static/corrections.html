Corrections Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Our Commitment to Accuracy

At SFAAM NEWS, accuracy is fundamental to our journalism. We are committed to providing reliable, factual reporting and promptly correcting any errors that occur.

2. How We Handle Errors

When we discover an error in our reporting, we:

  • Investigate the error promptly
  • Correct the article with a clear note about what was changed
  • Maintain transparency about our corrections process

3. Reporting Errors

If you believe you have found an error in our reporting, please contact us:

  • Email: editorial@sfaamnews.com
  • Subject line: "Correction Request"
  • Include the article URL and details about the error

4. Correction Standards

We distinguish between:

  • Corrections: For factual errors
  • Clarifications: When our reporting was accurate but could be clearer
  • Updates: When new information becomes available

5. Response Time

We aim to respond to correction requests within 24-48 hours and publish verified corrections as quickly as possible.

6. Contact

For correction requests, email editorial@sfaamnews.com with "Correction Request" in the subject line.
